Rules & Guidelines of Kerala Chat Room


Welcome to Kerala Chat Room - Malayalam Chat Room! Thanks for joining our Kerala's Friendly Chat Community. To ensure everyone has a great experience, Please Follow these Rules:


General Rules


  • ● Set a Display Picture (DP) after registering to earn rank badges.
  • ● Your ranking will be based on your activity level in the chat and forum.
  • ● Use a decent DP: No explicit or adult content.
  • ● Respect all users, especially girls and rank holders.
  • ● No abuse: Offenders will be kicked or banned.

Chat Etiquette


  • ● Keep personal details private: Do not share personal info or social IDs.
  • ● No sensitive topics: Avoid religious or political discussions in the chat room.
  • ● Use proper language: No excessive profanity, slang, or caps lock.
  • ● Stay on topic: Keep conversations relevant to the chat room's purpose.

Safety and Security


  • ● No sharing of explicit content: No images, videos, or links to explicit material.
  • ● No cyberbullying: No harassment, intimidation, or threats toward others.
  • ● No solicitation of personal info: Do not ask for or share personal contact info.
  • ● No discussing or promoting illegal activities: Keep conversations within legal boundaries.
  • ● Do not mention other chatroom names.

Chat Moderation


  • ● Respect moderators: Follow their instructions; they are here to help.
  • ● Report suspicious behavior: Inform moderators or admins of any suspicious activity.

Profile Rules


● Be honest about your age: Use your real age or hide it, but do not use unrealistic ages like 99, 98 or 97.

● Use Appropriate Profile Pictures (DP)


Consequences


Failure to follow these rules may result in being kicked or banned from the chat room, including temporary or permanent bans from the site.


Let's Keep it Fun and Safe


Enjoy chatting and have fun!
